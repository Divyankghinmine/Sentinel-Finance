# AI Finance Controller - Demo Scripts

## 🎙️ 90-Second Full Pitch Demo

**[0:00 - 0:10] Hook & Problem**
*Screen: Show a chaotic spreadsheet with thousands of unsorted rows.*
"Finance teams don't lose time because transactions are hard to record. They lose time because thousands of transactions *don't match* across different systems. Amounts differ by pennies, vendor names change, and dates are misaligned."

**[0:10 - 0:25] Solution Introduction**
*Screen: Switch to the AI Finance Controller Dashboard - Overview Page.*
"Meet the AI Finance Controller. It’s an autonomous engine that ingests your data from banks, ERP ledgers, and payment processors, and reconciles them using deterministic matching and fuzzy logic."

**[0:25 - 0:45] Data & Execution**
*Screen: Click 'Run Reconciliation' and show the progress bar.*
"Let's look at 100 synthetic transactions we just generated. Some match perfectly, some have amount variances, and some are missing. When we run the engine, it scores every potential match across 5 different signals: Reference, Amount, Date, Vendor, and Currency."

**[0:45 - 0:65] Results & Exceptions**
*Screen: Highlight the 'Reconciliation Metrics' and switch to the 'Exceptions' tab.*
"Instantly, we see our match rate and confidence scores. But the real power is in the exceptions. The AI doesn't pretend everything matches. It knows what it can reconcile securely, and cleanly separates what needs human eyes—like these duplicates or amount mismatches."

**[0:65 - 0:80] AI Explanation**
*Screen: Click on a specific Exception (e.g. LIKELY_MATCH) and show the 'AI Reasoning' card.*
"For every flagged item, the AI Explainer agent tells you exactly *why* it needs review. 'The amounts and dates match, but the reference ID is completely different.' Transparent, human-readable logic for auditing."

**[0:80 - 0:90] Close**
*Screen: Return to the Dashboard Summary.*
"The AI Finance Controller reduces manual reconciliation by 90%, eliminating audit risk and freeing your finance team to focus on strategic growth."
